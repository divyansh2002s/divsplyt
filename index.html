1. Participants

Add everyone who joined the event. Names must be unique. Spreadsheet chaos starts with duplicate Rahuls.

Quick Rules

  • Paid and included: add expense under payer, keep payer checked.
  • Paid but not included: add expense under payer, untick payer.
  • Included but did not pay: keep checked.
  • Not included: untick.
  • Same item, different payer: add another expense under that payer.
